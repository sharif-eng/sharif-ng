## 📌 Introduction

A practical phishing investigation exercise focused on analyzing suspicious email activity, identifying indicators of compromise, understanding attack techniques, and documenting investigation findings.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze suspicious email content and artifacts
- Identify phishing indicators
- Examine URLs, domains, and other indicators
- Map observed activity to relevant attack techniques
- Document evidence and investigation findings
- Practice a structured SOC investigation workflow

## 🔎 Investigation Focus

**Email Analysis • Suspicious URLs • Domains • Indicators of Compromise • Social Engineering • Attack Techniques • Investigation Documentation**

## 🧠 Skills Demonstrated

**Phishing Analysis • IOC Identification • SOC Investigation • Email Security • Threat Analysis • Incident Documentation**
